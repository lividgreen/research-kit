# Flow cards

The flow's cards are its records: one Markdown file each, YAML front matter the `flow` command owns, and a prose body the project writes.
There are four kinds — idea, question, task and finding — and this page is their specification: where a record lives and how its file is named, the front matter's form, every field of each kind and who writes it, and the states and the transitions between them.
[The flow](flow.md) is the tool: its commands, the index and the directions file beside the records, `flow.toml` and the check that refuses a record that does not hold together; [Getting started](getting-started.md) shows a record as the tool writes it.
Everything here is what `flow.py` does, and the kit's tests hold the field lists below to the script's schema.

## The files

`flow.toml` binds the records directory (`records/` by default), and the kinds each have a subdirectory of it, named by the kind's plural: `ideas/`, `questions/`, `tasks/`, `findings/`.
A finding registered by a document that `[finding] shards` lists goes under `findings/<paper>/`, the document's file stem, rather than under `findings/` itself.
A closed task — done or abandoned — is moved to `archive/` by its closing transition, so the live directories show the front of work; a closed idea or question stays where it is, with its state.
Two files stand beside the kinds: `INDEX.md`, generated by `flow index`, and `directions.md`, which the owner writes; the log is bound separately and may live anywhere.

A record's file is `<id>-<slug>.md`, as `task-0001-prove-the-lemma.md`.
The id is the kind's prefix from `[prefixes]`, a hyphen and a number zero-padded to `id_width` digits, numbered per kind from the highest id of that kind anywhere under the records directory, the archive and the shards included, so an id is never reused.
The slug is the summary lowercased, every run of characters outside `a-z0-9` turned into one hyphen, cut at forty characters; it is for the reader, and a file is read by its id alone (`flow check` accepts a file with no slug, and refuses one whose id does not match its name or its directory).

## The form

The front matter stands between two `---` lines and is the subset of YAML the script writes: plain scalars, single-quoted strings (a quote doubled inside), double-quoted strings with escapes, `null`, `true` and `false`, integers, block lists (`- item`) and one-level maps.
Every field of the kind's schema is present, in the order the tables below give, `null` where it is empty and `[]` for an empty list; a hand edit stays inside that subset, since `flow check` reads every record back and fails one it cannot read.

The body is prose, one sentence per line, which `flow check` enforces because diffs and the index depend on it; where `markdownlint` is on PATH, a write that adds a violation is reverted.
A body points at where its subject is STATED — a document's section, a module, a round — and never restates it, because the statement moves and a copy does not.
Transitions append dated notes to the end of a body, each `YYYY-MM-DD — label:` and, after one blank line, the text of the file given: `resolution:` for a task made or aborted, `refined:` for a question refined with `--body`, `returned:` and `unparked:` with their one-line notes.

## The fields

The tables give each kind's fields in the order its front matter carries them, and who writes each.
*Minted* is the transition that creates the record; *transition* names the ones that change the field after; *`flow set`* is the one command for a field the project may change at any time; *hand* is a field the project edits in the file.
Everything else is never edited by hand: the check does not read who wrote a field, but a hand edit that leaves a record without what its transition would have written fails it.

Every kind carries these:

| Field | Holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | the record's id, matching its filename | minted |
| `type` | `idea`, `question`, `task` or `finding` | minted |
| `summary` | one line: what the record is about | minted; `flow set` |
| `origin` | `current` for a record the flow minted, `legacy` for one imported from before it and written by hand, which exempts a question from the kill-criteria rule until its first round | minted; hand, for a legacy record alone |
| `source` | where it came from: a talk, a paper, `refine question-0001` for a residue minted at a refine, `make task-0001` for one minted at a make | minted (`--source`) |
| `created` | the ISO date of the minting | minted |
| `tags` | free words; `do-not-reopen` on a question lists it under "Settled, do not re-open" | minted (`--tag`); hand |

### An idea

`id`, `type`, `state`, `summary`, `source`, `consumed_by`, `abandoned`, `origin`, `created`, `tags`.

| Field | Holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `state` | `open`, `consumed` or `abandoned` | minted; `frame`, `kill` |
| `consumed_by` | the question the idea was framed into | `frame` |
| `abandoned` | a map of `date` and `reason` once killed | `kill` |

### A question

`id`, `type`, `kind`, `state`, `summary`, `derived_from`, `parent`, `size`, `kill`, `checked`, `parked`, `rank`, `why_now`, `first_move`, `direction`, `answered_by`, `abandoned`, `origin`, `source`, `created`, `tags`.

| Field | Holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kind` | `question`, or `conjecture` for one | minted (`frame --kind`) |
| `state` | `open`, `decomposed`, `answered` or `abandoned` | minted; `refine`, `answer`, `kill` |
| `derived_from` | the ideas framed into it, or the one finding it was framed from, or the question it was refined out of | minted |
| `parent` | the question it is a sub-question of | minted (`frame --parent`, `refine --subq`) |
| `size` | one of `[question] sizes` | minted (`--size`); `flow set` |
| `kill` | the kill criteria: what would count as an answer, or show it not worth asking | minted (`--kill`); `flow set` |
| `checked` | the ISO date the question was last looked at | minted; `rank`, `unpark`, `checked` |
| `parked` | `null`, or a map of `date`, `reason` and `revival` while set aside | `park`, `unpark`; cleared by `answer` and `kill` |
| `rank` | its place on the ranked list, or `null` | `rank`; cleared by `answer`, `kill` and `park` |
| `why_now` | why it is ranked, one clause | `rank --why`; cleared with the rank |
| `first_move` | its first step, or "none yet" | `rank --first-move`; cleared with the rank |
| `direction` | the bold name of a bullet of the directions file | minted (`frame --direction`); `rank --direction` |
| `answered_by` | the findings, or the done tasks drawn under it, that answer it | `answer` |
| `abandoned` | a map of `date` and `reason` once killed | `kill` |

### A task

`id`, `type`, `kind`, `state`, `summary`, `question`, `derived_from`, `requires`, `size`, `owner`, `drawn`, `dod`, `rank`, `why_now`, `first_move`, `rider`, `abandoned`, `origin`, `source`, `created`, `tags`.

| Field | Holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kind` | one of `[task] kinds`; a kind outside `question_optional_kinds` needs a question, and a kind of `decision_kinds` is owner-only work | minted (`--kind`) |
| `state` | `ready`, `active`, `done` or `abandoned` | minted; `draw`, `return`, `make`, `abort`, `kill` |
| `question` | the question it stands under, or `null` for a question-optional kind | minted (`refine`, `task --question`) |
| `derived_from` | the question it was refined out of, or the task whose make minted it | minted |
| `requires` | findings it needs before it is ready | minted (`refine --requires`) |
| `size` | one of `[question] sizes` | minted (`--size`); `flow set` |
| `owner` | the owner whose hand holds it while active; a decision kind is minted under the decision owner | `draw`; cleared by `return`, `make`, `abort` and `kill` |
| `drawn` | the ISO date it was drawn | `draw`; cleared by `return` |
| `dod` | its definition of done | minted (`--dod`); `flow set` |
| `rank`, `why_now`, `first_move` | as a question's | `rank`; cleared by `make`, `abort` and `kill` |
| `rider` | `true` while it rides the next piece of work handed to another owner | minted `false`; `flow set rider on` or `off`; `false` again at `make` and `abort` |
| `abandoned` | a map of `date` and `reason` once aborted or killed | `abort`, `kill` |

### A finding

`id`, `type`, `summary`, `status`, `evidence`, `derived_from`, `question`, `depends_on`, `paper`, `origin`, `source`, `created`, `tags`.
A finding has no `state`: it is a checkable claim, and it accumulates.

| Field | Holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `status` | one of the four roles `[finding]` names (`verified`, `stated`, `empirical`, `refuted` by default), which must agree with the kinds of evidence present | minted (`make --finding SUMMARY STATUS EVIDENCE…`) |
| `evidence` | one or more items of the forms below, each resolved when the finding is minted and at every check | minted |
| `derived_from` | the task whose make or abort minted it | minted |
| `question` | that task's question | minted |
| `depends_on` | findings it builds on; a cycle is refused | minted (`--depends-on`); `flow set` |
| `paper` | the document that registers it, which names its shard; read off a row or statement item when `--paper` does not give it | minted |

An evidence item is one of four forms, each resolved against what `flow.toml` binds:

| Form | Example | Resolved against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| declaration | `Lib/Foo.lean#lemma_L`, or a whole module `Lib/Foo.lean` | the declaration manifest of `[evidence] manifest`, a text file of `Module.Path name` lines; the file is looked up under `manifest_roots`, and the extension is `manifest_extension` |
| row | `papers/alpha.md#FV-1` | a table row of that document whose first cell is the id: two or three capitals, a hyphen, then letters and digits |
| statement | `papers/alpha.md#Theorem 2.1` | a numbered statement of that document: Theorem, Proposition, Lemma, Corollary, Definition, Remark, Fact, Conjecture or Example, then its number, at a line start, after `**` or in a heading |
| probe | `probes/p.py` | the probes register of `[evidence] probes`: a Markdown table whose first column holds the file's name in backticks |

The status follows the evidence: `verified` needs a declaration, `stated` a statement or a row and no declaration, `empirical` a probe and no declaration, `refuted` a guard declaration or a probe as the counterexample.
Where no manifest or register is bound, declaration and probe evidence is reported unchecked and the check's coverage line says so.

## The states and the transitions

A record's state changes only through a transition, which writes everything the new state needs and nothing a hand would have to add.
Each table below gives a kind's states, the transition between each pair, and what the transition writes beside the state; a transition refuses a record in the wrong state, so the tables are complete.

### An idea's

| From | To | Transition | What else it writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `open` | `capture`, or `refine --idea` and `make --idea` for a residue | `summary`, `source`, `tags`, a body where `--body` gives one |
| `open` | `consumed` | `frame` | `consumed_by`, the question minted |
| `open` | `abandoned` | `kill` | `abandoned` with its date and reason |

### A question's

| From | To | Transition | What else it writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `open` | `frame` from ideas or one finding; `refine --subq` and `make --question` for a residue | `kind`, `kill`, `derived_from`, `parent`, `size`, `direction`, `checked` set to today |
| `open`, `decomposed` | `decomposed` | `refine`, which mints at least one task and may mint sub-questions and ideas; a parked question is refused | a `refined:` note where `--body` gives one |
| `open` | `open`, parked | `park` | `parked` with its date, reason and revival; the rank cleared |
| `open`, parked | `open` | `unpark` | `parked` cleared, `checked` set to today, an `unparked:` note |
| `open`, `decomposed` | `answered` | `answer`, refused while a task under it is ready or active | `answered_by`; the rank and the parking cleared |
| `open`, `decomposed` | `abandoned` | `kill`, refused while a task under it is ready or active | `abandoned` with its date and reason; the rank and the parking cleared |

`rank`, `checked` and `flow set` act on an open or decomposed question without changing its state.

### A task's

| From | To | Transition | What else it writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `ready` | `refine --task`, `task`, or `make --task` for a follow-up | `kind`, `question`, `derived_from`, `requires`, `size`, `dod`; `owner` where the kind is a decision kind |
| `ready` | `active` | `draw`, refused when the owner's hand is full | `owner`, `drawn` |
| `active` | `ready` | `return` | `owner` and `drawn` cleared, a `returned:` note |
| `active` | `done` | `make`, which may mint findings, follow-up tasks, questions and ideas | a `resolution:` note; `owner`, the rank and `rider` cleared; the file moved to the archive |
| `active` | `abandoned` | `abort`, which may mint `refuted` findings | `abandoned`, a `resolution:` note; `owner`, the rank and `rider` cleared; the file moved to the archive |
| `ready` | `abandoned` | `kill` | `abandoned`; the rank cleared; the file moved to the archive |

`rank` and `flow set` act on a ready or active task without changing its state.

### A finding's

A finding is minted by `make`, with any status its evidence supports, or by `abort`, with the `refuted` status alone, and has no transition after: it is never killed, since a finding found false is refuted by another finding.

### The ranked list

`rank` places an open or decomposed question that is not parked, or a ready or active task that is neither of a decision kind nor held by a decision owner, at a place on the list; everything else is refused.
`flow rank ID N --why … --first-move …` gives one record a free rank, `--insert` takes a held rank and moves the records below it down by one, `flow rank --set ID…` fixes the whole order at once and clears every rank it does not name, and `flow rank ID --clear` clears one.
A ranked question's `checked` date is refreshed at each of these.
Every transition that clears a rank — `make`, `abort`, `kill`, `answer`, `park`, a clear — renumbers the records that keep theirs, so the list stays `1..n` without a hand on it.
